Legal Architecture

Governance & Safe Harbor

Compliance under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the FCRA 2020 Contrast.

Section 79 IT Act Intermediary Compliance

ACTA operates as an electronic platform intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. ACTA does not solicit, custody, or manage charitable funds directly. 100% of micro-donations are processed via RBI-licensed payment gateways directly into programmatic escrow accounts released strictly upon verified ground proof.

✓ Grievance Officer Appointed: grievance@resence.in • Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

The FCRA 2020 Paradox

Under the Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA 2020), foreign donations are strictly tracked via SBI New Delhi accounts. Yet India's ₹30,000 Crore domestic retail giving operates without proof requirements. ACTA brings cryptographic and hardware-level auditability to domestic giving, setting a new benchmark for national philanthropic transparency.

12. Practical Problems and Responses

NGO partners are difficult to secure

Start with a narrow use case, personalize outreach and focus on one realistic pilot partner.

Users register but do not return

Simplify onboarding, offer meaningful activities and measure repeat participation.

NGOs are unwilling to pay

Demonstrate value through a pilot and test willingness to pay before locking the pricing model.

Payment split is not approved

Confirm the exact flow with the provider and redesign the billing arrangement with qualified advice.

Fake evidence is submitted

Use reviewer workflows, timestamps, duplicate checks and audit trails; do not claim fraud can be eliminated completely.

Donation and campaign records do not match

Reconcile provider transaction IDs, gross amounts, fees, refunds and net settlements.

The demo or server fails

Prepare a tested demo account, recording, screenshots and a concise offline explanation.

Team disagreements occur

Document roles, ownership, weekly goals and decision-making rules.

The product scope keeps expanding

Start with one institution, one NGO and one campaign; defer advanced features.

Personal data is exposed

Minimize collection, restrict access, secure storage and backups, and prepare an incident-response process.

Campaign outcomes are not achieved

Report results transparently, explain what was completed and document lessons learned.

A competitor launches similar features

Improve execution, partner relationships, operations, usability and demonstrated outcomes.

The incubator does not provide funding

Use mentorship, partner introductions and feedback where available, and explore other funding options separately.

Users think ACTA is only a donation app

Demonstrate volunteering, institutional participation, evidence and impact tracking—not just donations.

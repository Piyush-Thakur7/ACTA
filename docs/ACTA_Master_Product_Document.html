01. Product Overview, Vision & Strategic Rationale

1.1 Project Specification

ACTA is an autonomous civic-tech network developed under parent entity Resence (https://resence.in). It directly resolves three structural dysfunctions in the Indian social giving ecosystem:

1. The Ground Proof Deficit (67.5% Trust Collapse)

Donors receive generic email thank-yous or 80G tax slips, but zero visual evidence that food reached a hungry stomach or a tree was planted. Independent research indicates that 67.5% of educated urban youth refuse to donate to online charities due to corruption fears. ACTA mandates geotagged video proof within 96 hours before escrow release.

2. The "Chillar" Stigma & Minimum Ticket Barrier

Traditional charities (Ketto, Milaap) ignore donations below ₹500, treating small sums as "chillar". A college student on a ₹3,000 monthly allowance feels socially embarrassed to give ₹20. ACTA normalizes and celebrates ₹10 micro-giving via UPI, where 1,000 students pooling ₹10 generate ₹10,000 to feed 285 people.

3. The Retention & Community Void

Charity in India is treated as an episodic, guilt-driven transaction. ACTA introduces an engaging collegiate habit loop—institutional campus communities, streaks, action-based experience points (XP), and dignified impact tiers.

1.2 Classical Latin Root & Historical Role

In Classical Latin, Acta translates to "Things Done", "Deeds Accomplished", or "Official Public Records." In 131 BC, the Roman Republic hung the Acta Diurna ("Daily Deeds") in the Forum as civilization's first daily public bulletin. On ACTA, every ₹10 donation is an individual Acta—a recorded, verified deed of human compassion, fused with the locked tagline: "Small Acts, Big Impacts."

05. In-App Camera Lock & Offline Video Sync Queue

5.1 Hardware Lockout & Canvas Telemetry

To eliminate pre-recorded stock video fraud, gallery uploads (<input type="file">) are permanently locked. Volunteers must film live via WebRTC (MediaDevices.getUserMedia). A canvas overlay injects an immutable cryptographic watermark containing: Campaign Batch ID, NGO Darpan ID, NTP-synchronized UTC timestamp, and high-precision GPS coordinates.

5.2 Offline Video Capture & Automatic Background Sync Queue

The Rural Slum & Poor Connectivity Architecture

Grassroots NGOs often distribute meals in remote slums where mobile data drops to zero. ACTA implements an offline-first capture pipeline:

// Client Offline Video Capture & Background Sync Queue Architecture 1. Local Storage via IndexedDB: As the volunteer records via the in-app camera, video blobs are chunked into 2MB fragments and encrypted using AES-GCM, stored in IndexedDB ('acta_offline_media_store'). Raw hardware telemetry (GPS bounding box, gyroscope motion) is stored alongside. 2. Service Worker Background Sync Registration: navigator.serviceWorker.ready.then((reg) => { return reg.sync.register('acta-sync-proof-bundle'); }); 3. Automatic Resume on Connectivity Restoration: The Service Worker listens to 'sync' and window 'online' events. The moment the device reconnects to 4G/Wi-Fi, the sync worker activates in the background, uploading chunks sequentially to pre-signed cloud storage URLs. SHA-256 hash is validated upon completion. 4. Volunteer Push Confirmation: Field lead receives confirmation modal: "Proof footage successfully synced to ACTA Cloud. Submission ID #88129 verified."

7.1 End-to-End Fund Lifecycle State Flow

7.2 Razorpay Webhook Idempotency & Deduplication

Every incoming webhook is verified via HMAC SHA-256 against Razorpay Webhook Secret. The event ID (x-razorpay-event-id) is checked against Redis with a 24-hour TTL (SETNX) to guarantee zero duplicate processing. If a student's app drops connection, Razorpay's payment.captured webhook reconciles the transaction within 60 seconds.

7.3 5% Platform Maintenance Fee Accounting & Legal Custody

  • 0% MDR Regulatory Moat: Under Section 10A PSSA 2007, UPI transactions incur ₹0.00 gateway cost. 100% of donor money hits the escrow vault intact.
  • Deduction Timing: The 5% maintenance fee is deducted only upon successful post-verification delivery from the gross campaign batch.
  • Legal Custody: Funds flow into a dedicated Virtual Escrow / Nodal Account under RBI Nodal Guidelines. ACTA never holds funds in unmonitored current accounts.

08. Trust, Verification, Human-in-the-Loop & Dispute Policy

8.1 The 2-Phase Ground Proof Bundle

Phase 1: Wholesale Supply Purchase Proof

Captured: At wholesale merchant / retail store.

  • In-app video showing purchased raw supplies (50 kg rice, stationery boxes, blankets).
  • High-res photo of merchant GST bill showing itemized quantities, GSTIN, and shop stamp.
Phase 2: Ground Distribution Proof

Captured: At actual field execution site.

  • In-app video showing service delivery (hot food served, trees planted, notebooks handed over).
  • Hardware GPS coordinates and NTP network timestamps burned into frame metadata.

8.2 Human-in-the-Loop Verification & The Two-Person Rule

Google Gemini Vision performs pre-screening: OCR receipt extraction, duplicate frame hashing (pHash), and face coordinate detection. However, no AI model can unilaterally release escrow funds. A certified Resence Compliance Officer performs final verification. For disbursements exceeding ₹25,000, dual authorization (Compliance Officer + Platform Lead) is mandatory via independent 2FA tokens.

09. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance & Volunteer Safety Shield

9.1 DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Architecture

Statutory Beneficiary Privacy Mandate

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) imposes severe statutory penalties for unauthorized publication of imagery of minors and vulnerable persons.

  • Minors (<18 Years Old): In all school kit distributions, orphanages, and slum educational projects, facial features of children are 100% blurred by default using OpenCV / MediaPipe Gaussian Blur (ksize=51, sigma=30).
  • Destitute & Hospitalized Beneficiaries: Faces of patients, destitute elderly individuals, and hospital files are automatically masked to safeguard human dignity.
  • Authorized Staff: Adult NGO field operatives and volunteer leads remain unblurred as official institutional representatives.

9.2 Zero-Liability Volunteer Safety Shield

ACTA operates strictly as a Technology, Escrow, and Verification Infrastructure Layer:

  • ACTA never directly recruits, dispatches, or manages individual volunteers.
  • Ground execution is conducted 100% by the verified partner NGO using their own registered staff and vetted volunteers.
  • The partner NGO assumes 100% legal, physical, medical, and insurance liability for all ground activities under signed legal MoUs.
  • Distributions are restricted to daylight hours (8:00 AM to 5:30 PM). Volunteers must wear official NGO identification badges.

12. Operational FAQ, 90-Day Roadmap & Incubation Asks

12.1 Real-World Operational FAQ

Q1: What happens if an NGO is executing in a rural slum with zero mobile connectivity?

The in-app camera records offline. Video blobs and telemetry are stored encrypted in local IndexedDB. The Service Worker Background Sync engine automatically queues and syncs the upload the instant the device re-enters 4G/Wi-Fi coverage.

Q2: What happens if a campaign's proof video fails to load on the student's phone?

The app detects player error and immediately displays an inspected frame thumbnail carousel (with burned-in GPS and time telemetry), accompanied by a direct button to watch on YouTube, and full itemized vendor GST receipts.

Q3: What if an NGO defaults on the execution deadline?

If the deadline expires without an approved extension, the escrow vault triggers an automatic 100% refund credited to all donors' virtual wallets. The NGO receives Strike 1 on their permanent record.

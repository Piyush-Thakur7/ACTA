## Communication Guidelines & Ethical Standards

When social-impact deliveries encounter real-world obstacles, donor communication must be **truthful, prompt, empathetic, and objective**:
1. **Never Conceal Real-World Delays:** Donors must know the ground status before they have to ask.
2. **Never Falsely Accuse or Speculate:** Explain what is objectively known (e.g. "supplies delayed due to weather" or "verification documents pending"). Do not accuse non-profits of criminal intent or fraud prior to formal legal review.
3. **No Unverifiable Promises:** Never promise a video or delivery date that has not been confirmed in writing.
4. **Beneficiary Dignity & DPDP Compliance:** Never disclose names, private medical details, or unblurred imagery of minors or distressed beneficiaries in operational alerts.

## Template Suite (7 Standard Operational Scenarios)

### Template 1: Delivery Delayed (Ordinary Logistical Delay)
* **Trigger:** Campaign execution deadline reached; partner NGO filed valid extension or ground execution delayed by weather/procurement.
* **Channels:** In-App Push Notification + Email + Campaign Detail Status Pill.
```text
Subject: Update on your contribution to [Campaign Title]: Delivery update and timeline

Dear [Donor Name],

We wanted to share a transparent update regarding [Campaign Title], which you supported with your contribution of ₹[Amount] on [Donation Date].

On-ground delivery was originally scheduled for completion by [Original Date]. However, our partner NGO, [NGO Name], has reported a delay due to [Verified Reason, e.g., severe localized waterlogging / delay in wholesale grain procurement].

We are truly sorry that the delivery could not take place on the initial schedule. At ACTA, our commitment is that every rupee is accounted for with verified proof before campaign completion. 

Current Ground Status:
• Supplies Status: [Purchased & stored / Procurement in progress]
• Revised Delivery Window: [New Target Date, e.g., Oct 14, 2026]
• Escrow Custody: Your contribution remains safely locked in the campaign escrow vault.

You will receive our next verified status update on [Date/Time of next update]. You can track real-time progress anytime directly in your ACTA dashboard under "My Donations & Proof."

Thank you for your patience and for making this small act count.

Warm regards,  
The ACTA Verification Team  
ACTA by Resence • Small Acts. Big Impact.
```

* **Trigger:** NGO missed execution window or submitted incomplete receipts without communication; formal 48h cure warning dispatched.

Subject: Important update: [Campaign Title] is currently undergoing operational review

Dear [Donor Name],

In accordance with ACTA’s strict verification policy, we have issued a formal documentation notice to [NGO Name], giving them a 48-hour window to submit complete, auditable proof or clarify the delay. 

What this means for your contribution:
1. Complete Fund Protection: No funds are released to the partner organization until verified proof is inspected and approved.
2. Clear Resolution Deadline: The organization must resolve the submission by [Date, Time].
3. Next Update: We will update you immediately on [Date] following the conclusion of this review.

We appreciate your support and remain committed to 100% verifiable social impact.

Sincerely,  
The ACTA Verification & Compliance Desk

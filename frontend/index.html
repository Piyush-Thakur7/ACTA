National Policy Question

Why Does Transparency Only Exist for Foreign Funds?

Under the FCRA Amendment Act, the government strictly regulates and tracks foreign donations to prevent misuse. Yet, domestic donations from our own Indian citizens still operate in a complete black box without real-time tracking or proof. ACTA solves this exact dilemma.

Foreign Funding (FCRA)

Government Monitored
  • Mandatory dedicated SBI New Delhi main branch bank account.
  • Live public dashboard reporting and audit trail.
  • Administrative expenses capped at 20% to avoid fund diversion.
  • Strict regulatory consequences for non-compliance.
✓ Strict accountability enforced by law for foreign donors.

ACTA Domestic Solution

Citizen Transparency
  • Micro-Donations from ₹10: Democratic giving for students and youth.
  • In-App Live Video: Ground volunteers record delivery via locked in-app camera (gallery uploads blocked).
  • Wholesale GST Bills: Linked vendor purchase receipts uploaded to donor dashboards.
  • Strict ₹95/₹5 Split: 95% direct to field aid, 5% platform maintenance.
✓ Full transparency brought to local domestic donors and student communities.

Verification Architecture

The 3-Point Proof Verification Engine

How ACTA guarantees zero fund diversion and 100% ground execution visibility.

📹

1. In-App Camera Only

Partner NGO head volunteers must record distribution videos directly using ACTA's in-app camera. Pre-recorded gallery uploads are strictly disabled to eliminate fake or recycled media.

🛰️

2. GPS & NTP Telemetry

Every frame is watermarked with immutable satellite GPS coordinates, high-precision atomic timestamp (NTP), and SHA-256 frame hash to prove exact physical presence at the distribution site.

🧾

3. Wholesale GST Invoices

Every distribution is matched with verified GST purchase bills from wholesale provision merchants, ensuring 100% of donor funds purchased supplies at bulk wholesale rates.

About ACTA by Team Resence

The Mission

ACTA was born out of a simple observation at GL Bajaj: students want to give, but they cannot afford ₹500 minimum tickets, and they don't trust black-box charitable accounts. By lowering the donation threshold to ₹10 and requiring ground NGOs to record live video proof via in-app camera with linked wholesale GST bills, ACTA makes giving democratic, joyful, and 100% transparent.

Operated under Team Resence (resence.in), ACTA is preparing for its physical incubation defense at Bharat Udyam Sangam 2026 on 31st October 2026 at the GL Bajaj Centre for Research and Incubation (GLBCRI).

Executive Governance & Legal Safe Harbor

  • Positioned as an automated SaaS technical intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Canonical ₹100 Split: ₹95 direct ground aid, ₹5 platform fee (retaining a 20% Continuity Reserve).
  • Partner NGO verification via NITI Aayog NGO-DARPAN and Section 12A/80G status.
  • Live prototypes accessible at acta.resence.in.

Policy Context

The FCRA Contrast & Domestic Giving

Under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Act, the Indian Parliament enacted stringent measures to ensure that foreign donations entering India are strictly accounted for down to the exact rupee, routed exclusively through designated SBI accounts with mandatory quarterly public filings.

The Core Question: Why should rigorous digital transparency exist only for foreign donations, while domestic contributions from everyday Indian citizens and students remain in an opaque black box?

ACTA bridges this exact governance and trust void. By requiring in-app live video recording, immutable GPS watermarking, and linked wholesale vendor GST tax invoices for every single delivery starting at ₹10, ACTA empowers domestic donors with institutional-grade proof.
